What BizVoid does

One product. The whole client lifecycle.

From the first search to the recurring revenue: every stage of finding, winning and serving a local-business client.

Find

Scan any zip code, postal code or city — in the US or abroad — for local businesses, filtered by category and “no website only.” Or look up one specific business by name. Results dedupe against leads you've already seen.

Diagnose

A live audit checks SSL, mobile, SEO, Core Web Vitals, local SEO, trust signals, tech stack and content depth, then grades it into one score with severity-ranked flaws. It even catches a phone number with the wrong area code.

New

Agent Opportunity Scan

See how customers reach a business, whether anyone answers after hours, and how many Google reviews complain about missed calls. Get the agents it needs, a 0–100 readiness score, and what you could charge — even for businesses with no website.

Pitch

AI drafts a cold email or DM that names the exact flaws and gaps it found and benchmarks the business against nearby competitors using real local counts. Pitch a website, AI agents, or both.

Proposal Documents

One click turns a pitch into a branded, multi-page proposal: cover page, severity-coded findings, cost of inaction, competitor comparison, proposal letter and next steps — signed with your details and, on Agency, your own logo.

AI Site Builder

Build a complete site from any lead using its real address, hours, reviews and photos. 91 themes — 41 classic, 50 with real motion — single- or multi-page, refined in plain English, and rebuilt to fix exactly what the audit found.

Private Review & Deploy

Show a prospect their actual new site, not a screenshot: deploy it privately to a shareable review link, then promote it to live the moment they approve.

Optimize

Apply real fixes, not a to-do list: security headers, schema, meta tags, FAQs, sitemaps. Preview every change free before spending a credit, on BizVoid-built sites or connected WordPress, Wix, Shopify and GitHub sites.

MCP & API

Connect BizVoid to Claude, GPT, Cursor or any MCP-compatible agent. Scan, audit, pitch, build sites, export leads as CSV or JSON, and draft AI agents from a plain-English prompt.
