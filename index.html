What BizVoid does

Six tools. One workflow.

Every stage of finding and winning a local-business client, in a single product.

Find

Search any city and surface local businesses with no website, or a site that's quietly costing them customers. Filter by category, zip code, and "no website only."

Diagnose & Optimize

A live audit grades performance, mobile, SEO, accessibility and security into one flaw score, then Optimize applies real fixes: security headers, schema, meta tags, sitemaps, even on sites BizVoid didn't build.

Pitch

AI reads the audit and drafts a cold email that names the exact problem it found and ties it to lost revenue. Grounded in real data, never a generic template.

Render

Turn the audit into a live, working HTML mockup of the prospect's redesigned site: a real preview they can open, built from their actual brand and content.

New

AI Site Builder

Generate a complete, ready-to-hand-off website from any lead: real category, services, hours, reviews and photos pulled from Google, not a generic template. Single- or multi-page, live-previewed, exportable.

MCP & API

Connect BizVoid to Claude, GPT, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible agent. One plain-English prompt finds leads, runs audits, and drafts pitches without opening the dashboard.
